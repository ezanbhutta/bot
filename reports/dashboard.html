Honesty notes.

  • Not modeled: liquidation mechanics, borrow limits, exchange outages at listing minutes. Slippage is a model (range-scaled, capped adverse fills) — reality can be worse.
  • A REAL verdict validates a signal, not a deployment. Everything here is read-only research output.
